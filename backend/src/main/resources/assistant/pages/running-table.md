**A domain as it runs** (opened from an application or a commit). Its tables exactly as deployed. Cells can be changed freely here to try ideas — nothing is saved and nothing is deployed; changes show in blue. **Try it** evaluates values; **Run the tests** runs its test cases on what is on screen.

**Put back what runs** (or **Undo my changes**) discards the changes. **Start a fix to** the tag **with these changes** (or **Start a work space with these changes**) turns them into a work space, where they can be saved, committed and merged.

**History**, above the grid, shows the commits up to this one that changed the table, or — once it is open and a cell is clicked — that cell (**Show the history of …**), its rule, its column, or the whole domain: who, when, the value before and after. It reads only when asked.
