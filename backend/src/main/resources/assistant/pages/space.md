**A work space's home.** Its domains (each a DMN file with one or more tables), which of them changed since the last commit, and the steps along the top: **Edit**, **Test**, **Commit changes**, **Try in dev**, **Merge** — the next step is the ringed one. At the top right: what it builds on and which branch it merges into.

Opening a domain opens its tables to change rules and test cases; that is where a rule or a test case is added. The button at the bottom right is always the next step (for example **Run the tests**, **Review and commit changes**, **Merge**).
